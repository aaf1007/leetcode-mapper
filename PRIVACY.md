# Privacy policy: Roadmap for LeetCode

Roadmap for LeetCode is a Chrome extension that shows a LeetCode problem list as a topic roadmap. It has no server of its own and collects nothing about you.

## What it reads

When you open the roadmap on leetcode.com, the extension asks leetcode.com for the problems in the list you're viewing: titles, difficulty, topic tags, acceptance rate, and whether you've solved each one. It makes these requests from the page you're on, using the LeetCode session you're already signed in with, the same way LeetCode's own page does. This information is only shown to you and is not sent anywhere else.

## What it stores

- **Your sort choice and the Tags toggle**, in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer.
- **Problems you move to a different topic**, in Chrome's sync storage, so they follow you to other computers where you're signed in to Chrome. Chrome syncs this through your Google account; the extension's developer can't see it.

Uninstalling the extension deletes this data.

## What it doesn't do

- No analytics, tracking, ads or third-party scripts.
- No data is sent to the developer or to anyone other than leetcode.com.
- No data is sold or shared.
